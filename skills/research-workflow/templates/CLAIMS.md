# Claims and evidence

Use this record to decide what the paper can state.
Each claim needs a scope and an evidence location.
Keep opposing evidence visible beside supporting evidence.

| Claim ID | Claim and scope | Type | Supporting evidence | Opposing evidence | Status | Limits | Next test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use `source claim`, `hypothesis`, `observation`, or `conclusion` for type.
Use `supported`, `opposed`, or `unresolved` for status.
For a source claim, support means that the source states it.
It does not mean that this project reproduced the claim.

Use paper IDs with section locations for external sources.
Use experiment IDs with result paths for project observations.
Mark claims based on synthetic data as practice only.
